Enjoy Traditional Games Responsibly

Traditional games can be a fun way to connect with Tamil culture. Use these simple reminders when learning about or taking part in organised activities.

1. Learn the Background

Before playing, understand the game's history, local name and purpose. Ask family members, teachers or cultural organisations to share what they know.

  • Listen to local variations and stories.
  • Give credit to the community that preserves the tradition.

2. Choose a Suitable Place and Time

Use a clean, open and well-lit space for suitable games. Avoid crowded areas, slippery surfaces and extreme weather.

3. Prepare the Equipment

Check that boards, tokens and other materials are clean and in good condition. Do not use damaged or sharp objects.

  • Keep small pieces away from very young children.
  • Store equipment safely after use.

4. Learn Rules with Guidance

Ask a teacher, parent or experienced player to explain the rules. For physical sports, warm up appropriately and follow age-appropriate instructions.

5. Put Safety First

Respect personal space, stop if someone feels uncomfortable and never pressure anyone to join. Martial arts and contact sports must be taught by qualified instructors in a supervised setting.

6. Respect Every Player

  • Take turns and follow agreed rules.
  • Encourage beginners and younger players.
  • Accept winning and losing respectfully.
  • Never tease or exclude someone because of their skill level.

7. Keep the Tradition Alive

Record elders' memories with their permission, organise school heritage displays and share accurate information about local games.

8. Be Responsible at Community Events

Follow event organisers' safety instructions, respect public spaces and leave the venue clean. Watch cultural demonstrations from designated viewing areas.

Simple Preparation Checklist

  • Understand the game's rules and age suitability.
  • Choose a safe and supervised place.
  • Check that equipment is clean and undamaged.
  • Carry drinking water for outdoor activities.
  • Wear comfortable clothing and suitable footwear.
  • Respect teammates, opponents and organisers.
Important reminder: Participation should always be voluntary, age-appropriate and supervised when needed. Safety and respect are more important than winning.
